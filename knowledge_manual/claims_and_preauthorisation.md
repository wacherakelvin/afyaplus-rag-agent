# AfyaPlus Claims and Pre-authorisation Procedures

> Fictional procedures document created for training purposes.

## When pre-authorisation is required

Pre-authorisation is AfyaPlus approval obtained before treatment. It is required for all planned
hospital admissions, all planned surgery, MRI and CT scans, physiotherapy beyond six sessions in a
policy year, and any single outpatient bill expected to exceed KES 30,000. Emergency treatment does
not require pre-authorisation.

## How to request pre-authorisation

The treating provider submits the request through the AfyaPlus provider portal. The request must
include the diagnosis, the ICD-10 diagnosis code, an itemised cost estimate and the doctor's clinical
notes. AfyaPlus responds within 2 working hours for diagnostic imaging and within 24 hours for planned
admissions and surgery.

## Treatment without pre-authorisation

If treatment that needs pre-authorisation goes ahead without approval, AfyaPlus may decline the
claim or pay only part of it. The member may appeal the decision.

## Submitting a reimbursement claim

Members who pay a provider directly can claim reimbursement within 90 days of treatment. The claim
must include a completed AfyaPlus claim form, the itemised invoice, payment receipts, the
prescription for any medicines, and a discharge summary for inpatient care. Complete claims are
processed within 10 working days. Incomplete claims are returned to the member with a list of
missing documents.

## Appeals

A member may appeal any claim or pre-authorisation decision within 30 days of the decision letter.
Appeals are reviewed by the AfyaPlus medical panel, which issues a written decision within 14 working
days.

## Fraud

Submitting false or altered documents is fraud. AfyaPlus will decline the claim, may cancel the
policy and may report the matter to the relevant authorities.
