# AfyaPlus Health Cover Plans (2026 edition)

> Fictional policy document created for training purposes. All amounts are in Kenya shillings (KES)
> per member per policy year unless stated otherwise.

## Plan overview

AfyaPlus offers three individual medical plans: Bronze, Silver and Gold. Each plan has separate
annual limits for outpatient and inpatient care. When a limit is used up, the member pays the
remaining costs for that benefit until the policy renews.

## Outpatient cover

Outpatient cover pays for consultations, laboratory tests, basic X-rays and prescribed medicines
from AfyaPlus network pharmacies.

- Bronze: outpatient limit of KES 50,000 per year. AfyaPlus pays 70% of each eligible outpatient bill.
- Silver: outpatient limit of KES 100,000 per year. AfyaPlus pays 80% of each eligible outpatient bill.
- Gold: outpatient limit of KES 250,000 per year. AfyaPlus pays 90% of each eligible outpatient bill.

The member pays the remaining percentage directly to the provider at the time of the visit.

## Inpatient cover

Inpatient cover pays for hospital admission, surgery, theatre fees, ward charges and medicines
given during admission.

- Bronze: inpatient limit of KES 300,000 per year.
- Silver: inpatient limit of KES 750,000 per year.
- Gold: inpatient limit of KES 2,000,000 per year.

Inpatient bills at network hospitals are paid at 100% up to the plan limit. Bills at non-network
hospitals are paid at 80% up to the plan limit.

## Diagnostic imaging

MRI and CT scans are paid from the outpatient limit at the plan's outpatient percentage. MRI and CT
scans require pre-authorisation on all plans. Ultrasound and plain X-ray do not require
pre-authorisation.

## Maternity cover

- Bronze: maternity is not covered.
- Silver: maternity cover up to KES 120,000 per pregnancy, after a 10-month waiting period.
- Gold: maternity cover up to KES 250,000 per pregnancy, after a 10-month waiting period.

Maternity cover includes antenatal visits, delivery (normal or caesarean) and postnatal care for
six weeks after delivery.

## Waiting periods

New members must wait before some benefits can be used: 30 days for general illness, 12 months for
pre-existing conditions, and 10 months for maternity. There is no waiting period for accidents or
emergencies.

## Where cover applies

Bronze and Silver plans cover treatment in Kenya only. The Gold plan covers treatment in all East
African Community member states, plus emergency treatment anywhere else in the world up to
KES 500,000 per year.

## Exclusions

The following are not covered on any plan: cosmetic procedures (unless reconstructive surgery after
an accident), infertility treatment, experimental or unproven treatments, and injuries from taking
part in professional sport. Dental and optical care are excluded on Bronze and Silver. The Gold plan
includes dental cover up to KES 30,000 and optical cover up to KES 20,000 per year.

## Emergency care

Emergency treatment never requires pre-authorisation on any plan. The treating hospital must notify
AfyaPlus within 48 hours of an emergency admission so the case can be registered.
