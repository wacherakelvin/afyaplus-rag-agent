# AfyaPlus Data Protection Policy for Digital Assistants

> Fictional internal policy created for training purposes, aligned with the principles of the Kenya
> Data Protection Act, 2019.

## Health data is sensitive personal data

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, health information is sensitive personal data. AfyaPlus
processes it only for a lawful purpose, only with the member's consent or another lawful basis, and
only to the minimum extent needed for that purpose.

## Masking before external processing

Before a member's message is sent to any external AI service, the assistant replaces personal
identifiers with placeholder tokens. Identifiers include phone numbers, email addresses, national ID
numbers, KRA PINs and AfyaPlus member numbers. The original values stay inside AfyaPlus systems and
are re-inserted only into the final reply shown to the authorised member.

## Logging and retention

Assistant audit logs store masked text only and never store raw personal identifiers. Audit logs are
kept for 12 months and then deleted.

## Cross-border transfers

Personal data is transferred outside Kenya only where appropriate safeguards are in place. Masking
identifiers before using cloud AI services reduces the personal data that leaves the country.

## Member rights

Members have the right to be informed about how their data is used, to access their data, to have
inaccurate data corrected, to object to processing and to request deletion. Requests are handled by
the AfyaPlus Data Protection Officer at dpo@afyaplus.example.

## Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to put members at risk, AfyaPlus notifies the Office of the Data
Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and informs affected members
without undue delay.
